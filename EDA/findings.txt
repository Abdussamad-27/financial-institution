Key EDA Findings:
1. Target imbalance: Approved loans (1) make up roughly 24% of the dataset, while rejected loans (0) make up roughly 76%.
2. Credit score has the strongest positive correlation with loan approval at approximately 0.42.
3. Requested loan amount has a negative correlation with loan approval at approximately -0.21.
4. Annual income and monthly expenses are highly correlated at approximately 0.88.
